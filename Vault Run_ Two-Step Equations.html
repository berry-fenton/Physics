How to crack a lock

  1. Pick the first move: undo the number being added or subtracted.
  2. Type the new number on the right side.
  3. Pick the second move: undo the multiplying or dividing, then type what x equals.

A clean solve adds 5 seconds and builds your streak. A mistake costs 5 seconds. Locks get harder as you go.
