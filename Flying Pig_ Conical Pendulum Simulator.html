🐷 Flying Pig: Forces & Circular Motion

A conical pendulum: the pig flaps around a horizontal circle on a string hung from the ceiling.

Keys: Space pause · C cut · R reset · P prediction mode. Prediction mode hides the calculated answers so students can predict, then measure with the lap timer.

Measurements & calculations

Model assumptions: the pig's center of mass moves at constant speed in a horizontal circle. Wing thrust pushes forward along the path and exactly balances air drag, so those two cancel. In the vertical/radial plane only tension and weight act.

Questions to explore

  1. How many forces act on the pig? Which one is the "centripetal force"? (Hint: is it a new force, or the job one of the existing forces is doing?)
  2. Turn on the tension components. Which component balances the weight, and which points toward the center?
  3. Double the pig's mass. What happens to the forces? What happens to the speed and period? Why?
  4. Make the angle bigger. Does the pig need to go faster or slower? What happens to the tension?
  5. Predict which way the pig goes when you cut the string, then watch the top view. Which of Newton's laws is this?
  6. Turn on prediction mode, measure the period with the lap timer, and compare v = 2πr / T with v = √(r g tanθ).
