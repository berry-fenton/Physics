How to defend the ship

  1. Shoot the alien ship showing the move to do to both sides.
  2. Shoot the asteroid showing the new number on the right side.
  3. Press Check on the console. Do it twice to solve for x and destroy the threat.

Stray shots are free. You can reload before you press Check. A wrong Check makes the threat close in 5 seconds faster. If it reaches you, you lose a shield. Lose all 3 and the mission ends.
