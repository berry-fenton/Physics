Rules & physics

Draft

Each team has $800 for the whole game and must draft 4 running backs and 8 defenders. Every player can be used once.

Momentum p = m × v    Price = p ÷ 10

Each play

The offense secretly picks one runner and a lane (Top, Middle, Bottom). The defense secretly places 2 defenders in 2 different lanes. Both defenders are used up, even one in the wrong lane. Teams switch sides after every play. There are 8 plays in all, 2 per quarter.

The physics

Direction matters. Runners move right (+). Defenders move left (−), so their momentum is negative.

Conservation of momentum. During the collision the only big forces are the players pushing on each other (internal forces), so the system's total momentum is the same just before and just after the hit.

Collision types. A tackle is perfectly inelastic: the players move together at v′ = ptotal ÷ (mR + mD). A bounce is partly elastic, with a coefficient of restitution e = 0.5. Kinetic energy is lost in both.

Impulse. J = Δp = Favg × Δt. Each hit lasts Δt = 0.20 s. The players get equal and opposite impulses (Newton's third law). The area under each force–time curve is the impulse.

After the hit. Friction from the ground is an outside force. It slows the players until they fall, which is why momentum is only conserved during the collision.

Note: in real football, whether players stick or bounce depends on how they hit, not on how close their momenta are. The ratio thresholds are game rules.
